# Proposta: peça e molde triangulares

Arte aprovada e integrada ao jogo. Criada com a ferramenta integrada de geração de imagens, usando a máquina existente como referência de estilo.

Os recortes ficam em `data/items/triangle_part_texture.tres` e `triangle_mold_texture.tres`. O produto usa o novo ícone tanto no item físico quanto nas interfaces que consultam ItemData/ShapePieceData. `world_icon_size = 40` mantém a peça no chão proporcional ao personagem, independentemente da resolução da imagem. O molde aparece nas mãos e na seleção da bancada, preservando a posição por direção e a oclusão atrás do personagem.

Validação: 102 verificações do primeiro ciclo sem falhas e `examples/triangle_art_test.tscn` conferindo recursos, escala, transporte, ícone da bancada e coleta. Captura: `docs/images/triangle_in_game.png`.

- `triangle_part_v1.png`: chapa triangular de ferro envelhecido, com três furos e detalhes de latão.
- `triangle_mold_v1.png`: bloco com cavidade triangular e três pinos correspondentes aos furos da peça.

## Prompt da peça

Create ONE inventory/pickup game sprite: a small solid TRIANGULAR MECHANICAL PART, point upwards, matching the muted rustic pixel-art machinery in reference image 1. Reference 1 is STYLE AND MATERIAL reference only, do not depict a machine. Graveyard Keeper-inspired cozy worn medieval workshop pixel art, dark stepped pixel outlines, chunky readable pixel clusters, restrained warm gray iron, desaturated brass bevel and tiny brown wear marks. The part is a compact thick cast-metal triangular plate, clearly filled solid triangular silhouette, approximately equilateral, with a recessed central triangular face and THREE simple small dark fastening holes near its corners. Not a jewel, not a magic rune, not a golden ornate amulet; no gears or filigree. Make it feel like a useful component manufactured in the factory. Front face nearly parallel to the screen, slight top-down view with a small visible dark thickness along lower edge. Top left lighting. Clean legible silhouette at 48-64 pixel display size; art should look like a deliberately drawn 64x64 pixel sprite enlarged with nearest-neighbor, not photorealistic or smooth vector. Centered single object with generous padding. Transparent alpha everywhere outside the object. No glow, no ground, no cast shadow, no border, no text, no surrounding props.

## Prompt do molde

Create ONE companion game inventory sprite: an EMPTY casting mold used to manufacture the exact triangular mechanical part in reference image 1. Reference1 defines the triangular geometry and pixel-art palette, NOT the output object itself. The output is a compact thick RECTANGULAR charcoal cast-iron mold block, with a deeply recessed UPWARD-POINTING TRIANGULAR cavity in its center. The cavity must be clearly EMPTY and dark, a negative impression that fits the reference triangular plate; three small steel cylindrical locating pins in the cavity correspond to the three holes in the reference part. Broad simple rectangular outer rim with four modest worn brass corner fasteners. Clearly distinguish this mold from the finished triangular part by its rectangular silhouette, darker charcoal iron and inset cavity. Rustic Graveyard Keeper-inspired workshop pixel art, simple chunky pixel clusters and hard stepped outlines, restrained gray iron, muted brass corners, a few brown wear marks. Match reference shading, top-left light, nearly front-facing slight top-down perspective with a small visible lower thickness. Legible as a 48-64px inventory icon; enlarged nearest-neighbor pixel-art aesthetic. Single centered object with generous padding on TRANSPARENT ALPHA background. No background glow, no floor, no cast shadow, no text, no molten metal, no actual triangular part inside, no decorative gears or filigree.
