# Balcão de pedidos

Sprite gerado com a ferramenta nativa ImageGen, usando a máquina de triângulos e o idle do jogador como referências. PNG transparente: `assets/textures/props/order_counter_v1.png`. O sprite antigo foi preservado em `assets/textures/props/shop.png`.

Recorte em `data/props/order_counter_texture.tres`, largura no mapa de 240 px. Aplicado em `entities/props/shop_node.tscn`, mantendo a interação de entrega. Cliente reposicionado 65 px acima do balcão e desenhado atrás dele; rótulo aproximado do conjunto.

## Prompt

Create ONE isolated game prop sprite: a compact customer order counter for a rustic mechanical shapes factory. References: image 1 is the machines' material and pixel-art style; image 2 establishes character scale/style. Make a waist-high wooden service counter, NOT a building or machine. Front view with slightly overhead RPG perspective, broad visible tabletop, horizontal front edge, no isometric diamond. Warm worn walnut planks, dark iron corner straps and rivets, restrained brass trim and central brass gear medallion. On tabletop a small brass service bell on left and a paper order ledger on right, center empty for deliveries. Front three solid wooden panels and small sturdy feet. No roof, tall back wall, people, crates outside footprint, signage, writing or readable text. Crisp pixel art, dark clean pixel outlines, earthy browns and gold, matching reference machines, simple readable at 230 pixels wide in game. Width about 2.2 times total height. One centered complete sprite with transparent padding. True transparent alpha background, no floor or cast shadow or glow. Only one counter, no variants or animation sheet.
