# Sprites do retângulo

Gerados com a ferramenta integrada image_gen a partir dos sprites do quadrado, mantendo ferro envelhecido, ferrugem e latão. O molde tem cavidade e quatro pinos. Arquivos finais em assets/textures/items/concepts/rectangle_part_v1.png e rectangle_mold_v1.png. Os recursos rectangle_part_texture.tres e rectangle_mold_texture.tres em data/items recortam as margens transparentes.

A peça usa o novo sprite no chão, nas mãos, no estoque e nas pilhas do balcão. O molde usa seu sprite na lista da bancada, no chão e nas mãos. A geometria de montagem continua sendo 240 × 120. O ícone no mundo tem largura máxima de 48, compatível com as novas formas.

## Prompt da peça

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: transparent pixel art game item sprite. ONE finished RECTANGULAR mechanical iron part matching reference square part materials and style. Horizontal rectangle exactly twice as wide as high, straight square corners, front face top-down without perspective distortion, very slight front metal thickness. Weathered iron gray, brass bolt holes at FOUR corners, small warm orange rust speckles, inset flat iron center. Crisp chunky pixel art shading with dark outline like rustic steampunk crafting game, readable at 48px wide. ONE object centered with transparent margins. No text, no floor, no ground shadow, no glow. Actual transparent background.

## Prompt do molde

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: transparent pixel art game item sprite. ONE RECTANGULAR casting MOLD matching reference square mold. Horizontal rectangle precisely twice as wide as tall, square corners, top-down face with very slight front thickness. Weathered gray iron and orange rust speckles, four brass bolts at the corners. Deep dark rectangular cavity with FOUR raised silver locating pegs inside. Must read as an empty mold with pegs, matching reference material/pixel density. Crisp chunky pixel art and dark outline, visible at 48px wide. ONE centered isolated object with transparent margins, no lettering, no floor, no shadow or glow, actual transparent background.
